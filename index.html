GOOD TO KNOW

Questions schools often ask.

Clear answers about the portal, apps and school plans.

Register your school arrow_forward
Which schools can use SchoolBridge?add

SchoolBridge is designed for schools that want one place to manage school records, academics and daily operations. The plans offer different student limits, from 150 students on Basic to unlimited on Enterprise.

What can I manage in the web portal?add

Manage student and staff records, parents and guardians, classes, subjects, lesson topics, results, attendance, events, timetables, subscriptions, fees and finance.

Are there mobile and desktop apps?add

Yes. The platform includes an Android mobile app and a Windows desktop app alongside the school admin web portal.

Where do I download the apps?add

After signing in, school administrators can find the companion apps in App Versions in the portal.

How does pricing work?add

Basic is free. Pro, Premium and Enterprise are priced per term, with different student limits and plan features.

How do I get started?add

Register your school and administrator details, then use your school workspace to set up classes, subjects and records.
