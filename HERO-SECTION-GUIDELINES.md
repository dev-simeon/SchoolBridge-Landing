# SchoolBridge Hero Section Design Guidelines

Reference article: [Website Hero Section Best Practices + Examples — Prismic](https://prismic.io/blog/website-hero-section)

## Core hero elements

- **Headline:** Make the main value clear quickly, using a short, memorable statement that fits the audience and the page goal.
- **Supporting text:** Add just enough context to explain what the product does and why it helps. Keep it brief and easy to scan.
- **Call to action:** Make the main action obvious and use direct wording. Keep one primary CTA; use at most one visually quieter secondary CTA when it serves a real alternative path.
- **Visual:** Show the product or outcome in a way that reinforces the message. Keep it relevant and make sure it does not compete with the headline or CTA.
- **Navigation:** Keep navigation clear and easy to use while letting the hero message remain the focus.

## Design and implementation principles

1. Lead with the school's benefit and outcome, not a list of product features.
2. Use clear visual hierarchy and enough whitespace to guide the eye from headline, to supporting copy, to CTA.
3. Keep the hero's essential content visible and usable immediately; do not make text, CTA, or key imagery depend on animation to appear.
4. Optimize hero images and any motion so the initial view loads quickly. Animation should demonstrate or support the message, not delay content or distract from it.
5. Use social proof only when it is real and verified. Do not invent school counts, ratings, testimonials, or customer logos.
6. Make the design responsive and check that the message, visual, and CTA remain clear on smaller screens.
7. Treat the hero as something to refine and compare through variants, while preserving accurate product information.

## SchoolBridge application notes

- Keep copy in simple English and aligned with real SchoolBridge capabilities across the school admin portal and student/teacher companion apps.
- Use the established SchoolBridge blue palette and existing product preview artwork unless a new asset is deliberately requested.
- Keep the primary action focused on registering a school; a secondary action can lead to platform details.
- Product mockups and labels must reflect the actual platform. Avoid placeholder metrics, fake data presented as proof, or claims about unsupported features.
- Preserve fast loading: prefer the existing optimized/local artwork, avoid large autoplay media, and honor reduced-motion preferences.
